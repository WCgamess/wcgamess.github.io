My Spitz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Who we are

My Spitz is a game made by WCgames. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not use the internet, does not collect or share any personal data, has no accounts, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

Local storage of progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. We never receive this data. Deleting the game or its data removes it. If your device backs up app data to your own cloud account, that backup is handled by your device's operating system provider, not by us.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions beyond those the operating system grants to every game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
